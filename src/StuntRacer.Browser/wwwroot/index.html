THE DRIVER'S BRIEFING

GET A GRIP.

Keep all four tyres on the ribbon. Brake before a bend, then get back on the power. Use turbo on the straights and carry speed over the crests.

BLOCK RUSH / NEON YARD

Drive through foam cubes to score. Follow the painted lanes and steer onto either branch to reach the goal before time is up. Use W to drive and A/D to steer, or use the touch pedals or a controller. Pause freezes the clock; Garage or Restart gives you a fresh set of cubes.

FLIGHT CONTROLS

Throttle up along the runway, then gently pull back above about 130 knots. Retract gear after takeoff. Use rudder to steer while rolling on the runway; use the ailerons to bank once airborne. Low speed or excessive pitch can stall. Land aligned with the runway, gear down, with a gentle descent. Air Start puts you already flying for immediate practice.

With Assisted handling, bank and hold to turn. Ease off to level the wings. For a tighter turn at supersonic speed, release afterburner and reduce throttle and speed first. No flaps are needed. Choose Direct below for full roll authority and aerobatics.

COPPERCLOUD RANGE / FLIGHT TRAINING

Launch airborne on a course toward the moving drone and the ground beacon. Stay near your initial heading for a first pass. When the dashboard says a prop is in range, press T, click or tap SCAN, or click the right stick. Scans are proximity-only; keep flying and wait for the scanner to recharge. Tag both once to clear the range. Garage or Restart starts a fresh attempt.

AIR TAG DUEL / AMBERLINE RANGE

Launch airborne and keep level for the first pass. Follow the bright Kite glider; the scoreboard shows its distance and direction. When it says SCAN now, press T, click or tap SCAN, or click the right stick. Tag on three separate close passes before time is up. Scans only record a point. If you fly below the practice floor, the round ends early. Pause freezes both aircraft and the clock.

WORLD FLIGHT ATLAS

In the Garage, choose a preset or enter signed longitude (west is negative), latitude (south is negative) and a heading. Set the departure, then start flight.

Once airborne, open Flight plan for GPS, heading hold and time compression. Take control or move a flight control to disengage. Autopilot never lands the plane.

The whole globe uses measured elevation and water data. Optional regional detail loads as you fly. Airport labels are landmarks, not modeled runways. For play, not navigation.

Checkpoint Run: clear the lime-green gates in order before the timer expires. Gates award extra time; recovery costs time. Time Attack records a clean completed lap and replays your best as a translucent purple ghost on later laps or a restart. Recovery invalidates that lap's recording. Ghosts never collide; recordings stay in this session and clear when changing track or game mode. This is not a Stunts replay-file importer.
