World Flight — compiled scenery data notice

The terrain uses USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), The National Map,
public-domain bare-earth elevation, projected and downsampled into game meshes:
https://www.usgs.gov/3d-elevation-program/about-3dep-products-services

Base relief uses NOAA NCEI ETOPO 2022 v1, 60 arc-second surface model:
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/etopo-global-relief-model

Water masks use Natural Earth 1:10m land and lakes (public domain):
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Runway threshold coordinates use public-domain OurAirports data. Strip
dimensions and elevation were checked against King County Boeing Field information:
https://github.com/davidmegginson/ourairports-data

seattle-buildings.json, san-francisco-buildings.json and seattle-roads.json,
when present, are modified databases © OpenStreetMap contributors. They are
available under the Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Building ways were filtered and projected; heights may be estimated. Highway
centerlines were filtered, clipped and projected; bridge deck heights are estimates.
These databases are not relicensed as game code.

receipt.json records the input SHA-256 hashes and the data revision. No
provider endorses the game; this scenery is not intended for navigation.
